Digi Carotene — Media Assets Checklist

One-page brief for launch media · Homepage + key inner pages · Deliver compressed files only

Compress everything before handoff — web-optimised images & videos keep the site fast and use less memory/bandwidth. Prefer WebP/JPG for photos, PNG for logos, short MP4 for reels.
# Section (heading) What to give
1 Hero (home mosaic) 9 images — same crop/feel, campaign/work photos (compressed WebP or JPG)
2 Our Clients (logo marquee) At least 12 client logos — same size PNG, transparent, ~360×100, compressed
3 Campaign visuals… (Visual Showcase) 6 images — compressed WebP/JPG
4 Creative Reels At least 5 videos + 5 poster images (one poster per reel) — short, compressed MP4
5 Growth Our Clients Can Count (Results) 3 case cards — client name, 1 real metric, short story (optional: 3 compressed images)
6 Testimonials / reviews At least 3–5 Google reviews — name, role, city, quote + link to Google Business Profile
7 Industry pages — “Clients We Work With in This Sector” 2–3 real logos per industry × 7 industries ≈ 14–21 logos (same PNG format; can overlap with marquee)
8 Team / Founder 1 founder photo + team photos (as many named people as needed) — compressed
9 About / Clients page Same real client logos as marquee (no fake brands)

Compression & delivery rules

  • Photos: WebP or JPG, target under ~200–400 KB each where possible; avoid huge uncropped camera files.
  • Logos: PNG transparent, shared canvas ~360×100, keep file size small (ideally under ~50–80 KB each).
  • Videos: Short MP4 (H.264), compressed for web; prefer under ~3–8 MB each; no raw 4K dumps.
  • Posters: One still image per reel, compressed WebP/JPG.
  • Only permissioned client logos and real metrics — do not invent proof.
