STRATEGY
How to Use This Document
This document holds the complete website copy for digicarotene.com, written to rank on Google in Hyderabad
and Bangalore, to be quoted by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), and to
double as source material for paid campaigns.
Every page follows the same structure so the web developer can drop it straight into the CMS:
  Page settings: URL slug, meta title (under 60 characters), meta description (under 160 characters), primary
and secondary keywords, and the schema type to implement.
 Heading tags are marked inline as [H1], [H2] and [H3]. Each page has exactly one H1. Never skip a level (no H3
directly under the H1).
 Text without a tag is body copy (<p>). Bulleted lines are <ul> lists.
 FAQ blocks are written answer-first in 40 to 70 words, which is the length AI engines and Google's featured
snippets prefer to lift. Wrap every FAQ block in FAQPage JSON-LD.
 The Paid Media Kit at the end of each page gives Google Ads responsive search ad headlines (30 characters
max), descriptions (90 characters max), and Meta ad copy. These are character-checked. Landing-page
message match is built in: the ad promise is the H1 promise.
 Anything in [square brackets] is a placeholder for a fact only Digi Carotene can supply. Do not publish
placeholders. Do not replace them with guesses.
Market figures quoted on pages are sourced in the appendix. Re-check any figure older than 12 months before
go-live and update the year.

Brand Analysis: What the Current Site
Says vs What It Should Say
 H2
 What is working
 The AEO and GEO positioning is a genuine differentiator. Very few agencies in Hyderabad or Bangalore talk
credibly about being cited by AI answer engines.
 The online + offline + PR offer is rare. Most competitors do digital only, or events only. "One accountable
team" is a real reason to choose Digi Carotene.
 The name story ("Carotene is a pigment. Warm, concentrated, used with intent") is memorable and should
stay.
H2
 What is holding the site back
 The core promise is missing. The brief says Digi Carotene is a results-driven agency where decisions are taken
on data, with 7+ years of experience and 300+ clients across the globe. None of those proof points appear
anywhere on the current site.
 The language is written for other marketers, not for buyers. Phrases like "semantic nodes", "zero-leak paths",
"crawl-budget and latency auditing" and "brand-governed authority" impress nobody who is deciding
whether to sign a retainer. A restaurant owner in Kondapur or a SaaS founder in HSR Layout needs to hear
what changes for their business.
 There is no proof. Case studies are placeholders, and the SEO page shows a "+280%" KPI that is not attached
to any client. Unverified numbers damage trust and can attract complaints. Remove them until real numbers
replace them.
 "Premium brands only" positioning narrows the funnel. Digi Carotene's actual client base includes
restaurants, salons, clinics, education and B2B companies. The copy below speaks to ambitious businesses of
every size, with a premium standard of work.
 No city targeting. Hyderabad appears only in the footer, Bangalore does not appear at all, and some pages say
Dubai. Google cannot rank a site for a city it is not clearly about.
H2
 Recommended positioning
Positioning statement: Digi Carotene is a data-led, results-first marketing agency from Hyderabad, serving
Hyderabad, Bangalore and clients across the globe. For 7+ years and 300+ clients, we have helped brands get
found on Google, recommended by AI, and chosen in the real world, and we measure our work in leads, footfall
and revenue, not likes.
Primary tagline: Found. Chosen. Measured.
Alternate lines for ads and social: "Marketing that answers to data." / "Every rupee, accounted for." / "Less
guessing. More growing."

Keyword and Local SEO Strategy for
Hyderabad and Bangalore
 H2
 How the site will rank in two cities
Google ranks a page for a city when the page is clearly about that city, the business has a verified presence there,
and other trusted sites connect the business to that city. The copy handles the first part. The rest needs action
outside the CMS.
  City hub pages: Two new pages (Digital Marketing Agency in Hyderabad, Digital Marketing Agency in
Bangalore) are included in this document. They are the main targets for the high-volume "agency + city"
searches and they link down to every service page.
 Service pages: Each service page names both cities in the meta title or description and has a short "In
Hyderabad and Bengaluru" section, so it can rank for "[service] in Hyderabad" and "[service] in Bangalore"
without stuffing.
 Use both spellings. People search "Bangalore" more than "Bengaluru" but AI engines and newer listings use
"Bengaluru". The copy uses both naturally.
 Google Business Profile: Hyderabad needs a verified profile with the exact same name, address and phone
(NAP) as the website footer. [Important] Ranking in the Bangalore map pack requires a real, staffed Bangalore
address. If Digi Carotene does not have one, the Bangalore city page can still rank organically, but do not
create a virtual-office listing; Google suspends these.
 Citations and mentions: List the agency on Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist and Justdial with identical
NAP. AI engines lean heavily on these directories when answering "best agency in [city]" questions.
 Reviews: Ask every satisfied client for a Google review that mentions the service and city in their own words
(for example, "they ran our Meta ads for our Gachibowli clinic"). Never script or incentivise reviews.
