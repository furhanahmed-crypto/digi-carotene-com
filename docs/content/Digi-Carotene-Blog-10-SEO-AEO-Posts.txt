H1: How Much Does Digital Marketing Cost in Hyderabad in 2026?
Quick answer: In Hyderabad, most small and mid-sized businesses spend ₹ 40,000 to
₹ 1,50,000 a month on digital marketing in 2026. That usually covers an agency fee of
₹ 25,000– ₹ 75,000 plus a separate ad budget. A single service such as social media
management or local SEO starts around ₹ 15,000 a month; one-time websites start near
₹ 30,000.
Why is there no single price for digital marketing?
Digital marketing is a bundle of services, and each one is priced differently. A salon in
Kondapur that wants Instagram reels and Google Maps visibility needs a very different
team from a college running an admissions campaign across Telangana. Four things move
the price most: how many channels you need, how competitive your industry is, how much
content must be produced, and whether you pay for ads.
The most common budgeting mistake is mixing up the agency fee with the ad spend. The
fee pays people to plan, create and optimise. The ad spend goes straight to Google or
Meta and buys reach. Always ask for the two numbers separately.

How much ad spend do you need on top of the agency fee?
For local lead generation in Hyderabad, a realistic starting ad budget is ₹ 30,000– ₹ 1,00,000
a month. Below about ₹ 20,000, campaigns often don't get enough data for Google or Meta
to optimise, so cost per lead stays high.
A simple way to set the budget is to work backwards from the customers you want:
1. Decide how many new customers you need each month (say 40).
2. Estimate your enquiry-to-customer rate (say 1 in 5 enquiries buy).
3. Multiply to get the leads needed (40 × 5 = 200 leads).
4. Multiply by a realistic cost per lead for your industry (200 × ₹ 250 = ₹ 50,000 ad spend).
If that number is above what you can afford, the fix is usually conversion, not cutting
spend: a better landing page, faster WhatsApp follow-up, or a stronger offer.

Why do some agencies charge ₹ 8,000 and others ₹ 2,00,000?
Cheap packages usually mean templated posts, no custom shoots, no ad strategy and
junior staff handling many accounts. Premium pricing should buy senior strategy, original
content, conversion work and proper tracking. The question to ask is not "Which is
cheaper?" but "What will it cost me to win one customer?" An agency that costs twice as
much but halves your cost per customer is the cheaper option.
How can a small business start on a limited budget?
Start where intent is highest. For most local businesses that means a fully optimised
Google Business Profile, a fast landing page, and one paid channel (Google Search for
urgent needs, Meta for visual or impulse purchases). Add social media content and SEO
once leads are coming in. Explore what each service includes on our services page.

H1: Performance Marketing vs Growth Marketing: What's the Difference and
Which Do You Need?
Quick answer: Performance marketing uses paid ads on Google, Meta, YouTube or
LinkedIn to win customers now, measured by cost per lead and return on ad spend
(ROAS). Growth marketing improves the whole funnel — conversion, retention and
referrals — so each customer costs less and buys more often. Most businesses need
performance marketing first, then growth marketing to make it profitable.
What is performance marketing?
Performance marketing is advertising you pay for and measure by outcomes: leads, sales,
app installs or bookings. You set a budget, target an audience, and optimise every week
based on what each rupee returns. Typical channels are Google Search and Performance
Max, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), YouTube, LinkedIn and click-to-WhatsApp ads.
The core metrics are cost per lead (CPL), cost per acquisition (CPA) and ROAS. Its
strength is speed: a well-built campaign can bring enquiries within days. Its weakness is
that results stop when the budget stops.
What is growth marketing?
Growth marketing is a system for growing revenue across the full customer journey, not
only the first click. It runs structured experiments on offers, landing pages, follow-up,
onboarding, retention and referrals, then scales what works.
Its metrics are customer acquisition cost (CAC), conversion rate, repeat purchase rate
and customer lifetime value (LTV). Its strength is compounding: every improvement keeps
paying off. Its weakness is that it takes longer to show a headline number.

Performance marketing vs growth marketing at a glance
Which one does your business need first?
Choose based on where your growth is leaking:
Not enough enquiries? Start with performance marketing. You need traffic before you
can improve conversion.
Enough enquiries but few sales? Add growth marketing at the conversion stage: faster
WhatsApp replies, better landing pages, clearer offers.
Customers buy once and disappear? Growth marketing at the retention stage:
reminders, loyalty offers and win-back messages.
Ad costs rising every month? Both. Better creatives and targeting (performance) plus
higher conversion and repeat rates (growth) bring CAC down.
Why do the two work best together?
Performance marketing fills the funnel; growth marketing stops it leaking. Imagine a clinic
in Hyderabad paying ₹ 300 per lead with 1 in 10 leads booking. Each patient costs ₹ 3,000. If
WhatsApp follow-up lifts bookings to 1 in 6, the same ads now cost ₹ 1,800 per patient — a
40% drop without spending more on ads.

What does a growth marketing experiment look like?
A good experiment has one change, one metric and a fixed time window. Examples:
1. Replace a "Contact us" form with a click-to-WhatsApp button and measure enquiry-to-

3. Send a WhatsApp reminder 30 days after purchase and track the repeat order rate.
Run three or four tests a month, keep the winners and drop the rest.
FAQs
Is performance marketing the same as digital marketing? No. Performance marketing is
the paid, results-measured part of digital marketing. Digital marketing also includes SEO,
content, social media, email and websites.
Is growth hacking the same as growth marketing? They overlap. Growth hacking usually
means quick, low-cost tricks; growth marketing is a sustained, data-led process across
the full funnel.
What is a good ROAS? It depends on your margins. A business with 50% gross margin
breaks even at 2x ROAS; most aim for 3–4x. Lead-generation businesses track cost per
qualified lead instead.
Can a small business afford growth marketing? Yes. Many growth fixes cost little: faster
follow-up, a better landing page or a WhatsApp reminder flow. They often save more than
they cost within a month.

H1: Meta Ads vs Google Ads: Which Is Better for Small Businesses in India?
Quick answer: Google Ads works best when people already search for what you sell —
"dermatologist near me" or "CA firm in Hyderabad". Meta Ads (Instagram and Facebook)
works best when you need to create demand with visuals — food, fashion, salons, events
and offers. Most growing businesses use both: Google to capture intent, Meta to build it.
What is the core difference between Google Ads and Meta Ads?
Google Ads is intent-based. Your ad appears when someone types a search, so they
already want a solution. Meta Ads is interest-based. Your ad appears in feeds, Reels and
Stories for people who match an audience, whether or not they were looking today.
Think of Google as a shop on a busy street where people walk in asking for something.
Meta is a stall at a crowded mall: people weren't looking for you, but a good display stops
them.

H1: How to Reduce Cost Per Lead on Meta Ads: 12 Fixes That Work in 2026
Quick answer: To reduce cost per lead on Meta ads, test more creatives (especially
Reels), fix tracking with the Conversions API, simplify your audience so the algorithm can
learn, make the offer clearer, and follow up leads on WhatsApp within five minutes. Most
high CPL problems come from weak creative, broken tracking or slow follow-up — not
from the budget.
Why is my cost per lead on Meta so high?
High cost per lead (CPL) usually has one of five causes: creatives people scroll past, an
audience too narrow for the algorithm to learn, broken or missing conversion tracking, a
weak or unclear offer, or a landing page or form that loses people. Diagnose before you
change anything — check click-through rate (creative), cost per click (audience and
auction), and conversion rate (offer and page).
Fixes for creative
1. Test 4–6 new creatives every two weeks. Creative is now the biggest targeting lever on
Meta. Fresh angles prevent fatigue, which shows up as rising frequency and falling click-
through rate.
2. Lead with Reels. Short vertical video with a hook in the first two seconds usually beats
static images for reach and cost. Show the product, the result or the person — real faces
from your business outperform stock footage.
3. Make each creative a different idea, not a different colour. Test a price offer, a
testimonial-style story, a behind-the-scenes clip and a problem–solution demo. Small
design tweaks rarely move CPL.
Fixes for targeting and structure
4. Go broader. Advantage+ audiences and broad targeting often lower CPL because the
algorithm has room to find buyers. Keep location and age limits; drop stacked interests.
5. Consolidate campaigns. Five ad sets with ₹ 300 a day each learn slower than one with

H1: NMC Advertising Guidelines 2026: What Doctors, Clinics and Hospitals Can
(and Can't) Do Online
Quick answer: On 6 October 2026, the National Medical Commission issued guidelines on
ethical advertising that took effect immediately. Doctors cannot self-promote, solicit
patients, use testimonials, before-and-after photos, "best" or "No.1" claims, or AI-
generated promotional campaigns. Hospitals may share factual, verifiable information.
Agencies and influencers acting for a doctor do not shift the responsibility.
This article explains the guidelines for marketing planning and is not legal advice.
What did NMC announce?
The Ethics and Medical Registration Board of the NMC released the "Guidelines on
Ethical Advertising and Public Communication" through a public notice dated 6 October
2026, effective immediately. They were framed in light of a Supreme Court writ petition
and the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations,
2002 (Medical Dialogues; full guidelines PDF).
Advertising is defined broadly: any communication that directly or indirectly promotes a
doctor's or institution's services, reputation, qualifications or facilities — including
content created with AI or published through an agency or influencer (The South First).

What can hospitals and medical institutions advertise?
Hospitals may share factual, objective and verifiable information: name, location, contact
details, departments, facilities, equipment, emergency services, accreditation and
charges. They may not include patient solicitation, inducements, superiority claims, cure
guarantees or promotional portrayal of an individual doctor. A website directory listing
doctors' names, qualifications, specialties, registration details and availability is treated
as patient information, not advertising. Hospitals are also governed by the applicable
Clinical Establishments Act or state law.
What does this mean for digital marketing agencies?
Two provisions matter directly. First, using an agency, influencer or platform does not
absolve the doctor or hospital of responsibility for content they authorise. Second,
agency payments must not be structured so that payment is linked to procuring or
referring individual patients — which affects pay-per-patient or pay-per-appointment
models for doctors. Clinics should review agency contracts now.
What are the penalties?
State Medical Councils can take graded action against doctors after a show-cause notice:
a warning and ethics training for a first violation, censure and a monetary penalty for a
second, and suspension of registration for repeated or serious violations, up to removal
from the register for one to three years. Doctors can appeal to the NMC's Ethics and
Medical Registration Board within 60 days.
A compliant marketing plan for clinics and hospitals
1. Audit existing content. Remove testimonials, before-and-after posts, superiority
claims and inducement offers from Instagram, YouTube, Google Business Profile and
your website.
2. Add disclosures. Include doctors' qualifications and registration numbers on posts and
profile pages.
3. Lead with education. Build content around conditions, prevention and what to expect
— the kind of material patients and AI search both value.
4. Strengthen factual visibility. Keep Google Business Profile, departments, timings,
facilities and fees accurate and complete. Local SEO is factual information, not
solicitation.

FAQs
Can doctors still use Instagram in India? Yes, for public health education and factual
information, with name, qualifications and registration number disclosed. Self-promotion,
testimonials and patient solicitation are not permitted.
Can hospitals run Google or Meta ads? The guidelines allow hospitals to disseminate
factual, verifiable institutional information and leave the detail to state clinical

Are Google reviews allowed? Reviews that patients leave on their own are not prohibited,
but doctors and hospitals must not procure, manipulate or buy reviews, or use them as
promotional testimonials.
Do the guidelines apply to AI-generated content? Yes. AI-generated promotional
campaigns for commercial gain are prohibited, and permitted AI content must be marked
as AI-generated.
