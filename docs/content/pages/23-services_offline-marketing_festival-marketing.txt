H2
 What is festival marketing?
Festival marketing is the planning of campaigns around cultural and religious festivals, such as Sankranti, Ugadi,
Ganesh Chaturthi, Dasara, Diwali, Eid and Christmas, using themed creatives, offers, on-ground activations,
sponsorships and digital campaigns to connect with audiences when emotional engagement and purchase intent
are at their highest.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
When should we start planning festive campaigns?
Start at least 8 to 12 weeks before the festival. Pandal sponsorships, premium media and influencer slots
for Ganesh Chaturthi, Dasara and Diwali often book out a month or more in advance.
H3
How do you avoid culturally insensitive campaigns?
Our team is rooted in Hyderabad and Bangalore. We research each festival's traditions, run creatives past
native-language reviewers and avoid using sacred symbols casually. When in doubt, we choose respect
over shock value.
H3
Can small businesses benefit from festival marketing?
Yes. Local festivals are ideal for small businesses, with society stalls, neighbourhood pandal sponsorships,
WhatsApp offers and hyper-local ads delivering strong returns on modest budgets.
