 What is a campus activation?
A campus activation is a brand engagement programme run inside or around colleges and universities, through
fest sponsorships, branded stalls, workshops, competitions, sampling and student ambassador networks, to
build awareness, drive trials, app downloads or sign-ups, and create authentic word of mouth among students.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
What kinds of brands do well with campus activations?
Food and beverage, fashion, telecom, fintech, banking, edtech, apps, gaming, personal care, two-wheelers
and recruiting employers see strong results because students are actively forming habits and choosing
brands for the first time.
H3
When is the best time for campus activations?
Fest season, orientation weeks and the weeks before exams start are the best windows. We avoid exam
periods and holidays. Most college fests in Hyderabad and Bangalore cluster between January and April
and again around August to October.
H3
How do you measure campus campaign results?
Through unique referral codes, app installs, QR sign-ups, ambassador leaderboards, social content reach
and sales from campus-specific offers, all reported by college.
