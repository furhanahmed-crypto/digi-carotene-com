H2
 What is influencer marketing?
Influencer marketing is a collaboration between a brand and content creators who have engaged audiences on
platforms like Instagram and YouTube, where the creator promotes the brand through authentic content such as
reviews, reels, visits or event coverage. Effective campaigns match creator audiences to the brand's buyers and
track results with links and codes.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How do you choose the right influencers?
We check audience location, age and gender split, engagement rate, comment quality, follower
authenticity, past brand work and content style. A creator is only shortlisted if their audience closely
matches your actual buyers in Hyderabad, Bangalore or your target market.
H3
Are micro-influencers better than big influencers?
For local businesses and niche products, often yes. Micro-influencers (roughly 10,000 to 100,000
followers) usually have more engaged, local audiences and lower costs. Larger creators suit mass
awareness and big launches.
H3
Do influencer posts need to be disclosed?
Yes. In India, ASCI guidelines require paid or gifted collaborations to be clearly disclosed with labels like
#ad, #collab or #sponsored. We ensure every campaign follows these rules.
