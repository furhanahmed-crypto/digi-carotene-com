H2
 Your reputation is already online. The question is who's writing it.
Founders, doctors, consultants and executives are often the most trusted asset a business has, and the least
visible one. A thoughtful LinkedIn presence, a few strong media mentions and a clear story can open doors that
ad budgets never will. Done badly, it reads as self-promotion. Done well, it reads as expertise.
H2
 What is personal branding?
Personal branding is the strategic process of defining and communicating an individual's expertise, values and
story across platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, podcasts, media and search, so the right audience recognises
and trusts them. For founders and professionals, a strong personal brand drives business opportunities, hiring,
partnerships and investor confidence.

Do I have to write my own posts?
No. We interview you, capture your opinions and stories, and write posts in your voice. You review and

How long does it take to build a personal brand on LinkedIn?
Most people see noticeably better engagement and inbound conversations within 8 to 12 weeks of
consistent posting. Building recognised authority in your industry usually takes 6 to 12 months.

Not at all. Doctors, consultants, small business owners and mid-career professionals often benefit the
most, because a strong personal brand directly brings them clients, referrals and opportunities.
