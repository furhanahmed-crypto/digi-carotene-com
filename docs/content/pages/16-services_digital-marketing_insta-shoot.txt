 Great content is planned before the camera is switched on
The difference between a reel that gets 500 views and one that gets 50,000 is rarely the camera. It is the hook in
the first two seconds, the pacing, the on-screen text and whether it feels native to the platform. Our shoots start
with scripts and shot lists built from what is actually performing in your category.
H2
 What is an Instagram content shoot?
An Instagram content shoot is a planned photo and video production session designed specifically for social
media formats such as Reels, Stories and carousels. It typically includes concept and script development, a shot
list, on-location filming with mobile-first framing, and editing with hooks, captions, transitions and trending
audio, delivering a batch of ready-to-post content.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How many reels can you shoot in one day?
In a well-planned full-day shoot we typically capture footage for 8 to 15 reels plus a set of photos,
depending on locations, outfit changes and complexity. A half-day shoot usually covers 4 to 8 reels.
H3
Do I need to provide models or talent?
Not necessarily. Many of the best-performing reels feature founders, staff or real customers. If you need
models, anchors or creators, we can source and manage them for you.
H3
Do you also post the content?
Yes. Shoots can be booked on their own or bundled with our social media management, where we plan,
publish and track the content's performance.
