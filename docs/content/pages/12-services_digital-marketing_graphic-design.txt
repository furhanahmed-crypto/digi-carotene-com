H2
 What is graphic design for marketing?
Graphic design for marketing is the creation of visual assets (logos, brand identities, social media posts, ads,
brochures, packaging and signage) that communicate a brand's message and drive a specific action. Effective
marketing design balances brand consistency with clarity, so audiences recognise the brand and understand the
offer instantly.

 Designing for Hyderabad and Bangalore markets

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How long does a logo and brand identity take?
A complete brand identity, including logo, colours, fonts and guidelines, typically takes two to four weeks,
depending on research depth and revision rounds. Individual creatives like social posts or ads are usually
delivered within two to five working days.
H3
Do I get the source files?
Yes. Once the project is complete and paid for, you receive the final source files and full usage rights to
the approved designs.
H3
Can you design ads that perform better?
Yes. We design multiple variations per campaign, testing hooks, visuals, offers and formats, and use the
results from your ad accounts to guide the next round. Design decisions are backed by click and
conversion data.
