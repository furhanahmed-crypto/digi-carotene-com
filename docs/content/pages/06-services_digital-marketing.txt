H3
How do you measure digital marketing results?
We agree on business targets first, such as leads, bookings, sales or cost per acquisition. Then we track
them through GA4, ad platform conversions, call tracking and your CRM. You get a live dashboard and a
monthly report that explains what happened and what we will change.
H3
Is ad spend included in your fees?
No. Agency fees cover strategy, creative, management and reporting. Ad spend is paid directly to Google,
Meta or other platforms from your account, so you keep full ownership and transparency of every rupee.
