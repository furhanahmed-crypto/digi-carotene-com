H2
 What is a residential activation?
A residential activation is a brand event or stall set up inside a gated community, apartment complex or
residential township, usually with the approval of the residents' welfare association. It allows brands to
demonstrate products, offer trials, run health camps or workshops, and capture leads from households in a
trusted, uncluttered environment.

H3
Do you get permission from the society?
Yes. All residential activations are run with written approval from the residents' welfare association or
facility management. We never run unauthorised door-to-door campaigns.
H3
How many societies should I cover?
For a local service, three to five well-matched societies near your location over a month usually gives a
clear read on response. Larger brands often run rolling programmes across 20 or more communities.
