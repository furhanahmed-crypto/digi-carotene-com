H2
 What does a PR agency do?
A PR (public relations) agency manages how a brand or person is perceived by the public, media and
stakeholders. It secures earned media coverage, writes and distributes press releases, positions founders as
experts, manages crises and monitors reputation. Modern PR also strengthens search visibility and AI citations
through authoritative mentions.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How is PR different from advertising?
Advertising is paid space where you control the message. PR earns coverage through journalists and
publications that choose to write about you, which makes it more credible. Earned media also stays
searchable and is often used as a source by AI answer engines.
H3
Can you guarantee media coverage?
No ethical PR agency can guarantee editorial coverage, because journalists decide what to publish. We
improve your chances significantly with strong story angles, timing and relationships, and we report every
pitch and placement transparently.
H3
What is online reputation management?
Online reputation management is the monitoring and improvement of what people find about a brand or
person online, including reviews, search results, social mentions and AI answers. It involves responding to
reviews, publishing accurate positive content and addressing misinformation.
