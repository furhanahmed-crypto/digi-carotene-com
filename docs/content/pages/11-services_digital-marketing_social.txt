H2
 Posting every day isn't a strategy
Many brands post daily and still struggle to get a single enquiry from Instagram. The reason is simple: the
content is made for the brand, not for the audience. Platforms reward what people watch to the end, save and
send to a friend. That takes research, sharp hooks and constant testing, not just a calendar full of festival
greetings.
H2
 What does a social media marketing agency do?
A social media marketing agency plans, creates, publishes and manages a brand's content on platforms like
Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, engages with the community, runs paid social campaigns, and
measures performance. Digi Carotene measures social success in reach quality, saves, shares, enquiries and
sales, not follower count alone.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
Which social media platform is best for my business?
Instagram suits food, fashion, beauty, lifestyle, real estate and education. LinkedIn suits B2B, SaaS and
personal branding. YouTube suits long-form education and high-consideration products. Facebook still
works well for local, family-focused audiences. We recommend platforms based on where your buyers
are, not trends.
H3
How many posts per month do you create?
Packages typically include 12 to 30 pieces a month, with a mix of reels, carousels, stories and statics. We
recommend fewer, better posts with strong reels over high-volume, low-impact posting.
H3
Can social media actually generate leads?
Yes, when it is built for it. Clear calls to action, link-in-bio landing pages, click-to-WhatsApp ads, lead forms
and fast DM replies turn engagement into enquiries. We track every lead source so you can see what
social contributes.
