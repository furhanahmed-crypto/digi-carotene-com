 Content that answers beats content that announces
Most brand content talks about the brand: our launch, our award, our team outing. Customers care about their
problem. AI answer engines care about clear, trustworthy answers. Content that leads with real questions earns
rankings, citations, shares and, eventually, sales.
H2
 What is content marketing?
Content marketing is the practice of creating and distributing useful content (articles, guides, videos, social posts,
case studies and emails) that answers a target audience's questions and builds trust over time, so that when they
are ready to buy, they choose your brand. It powers SEO, AEO, social media and sales conversations.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How often should a business publish content?
Consistency matters more than volume. For most businesses, two to four well-researched articles a
month, repurposed into social and email content, outperform daily low-quality posts. Digi Carotene sets
the frequency based on your competition and goals.
H3
Do you use AI to write content?
We use AI for research and drafting support, but every piece is planned, written and edited by
experienced writers and fact-checked. Google and AI engines reward content that shows real expertise, so
human judgement stays at the centre.
H3
What kind of content gets cited by AI search engines?
Content that directly answers a specific question in the first few lines, uses clear headings, includes facts
with sources, and comes from a brand that is consistently described across the web. FAQ sections,
comparison tables and definitions are especially likely to be quoted.
