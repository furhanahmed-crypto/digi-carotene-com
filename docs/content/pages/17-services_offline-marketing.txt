H2
 Offline is not old-school. Unmeasured offline is.
Out-of-home was the only traditional medium in India to grow in 2025, while feeds keep getting more crowded
and more expensive. The problem with offline was never reach; it was proof. We fix that with QR journeys,
WhatsApp opt-ins, unique offer codes, lead capture tablets and follow-up retargeting, so footfall becomes a
number in your dashboard, not a guess in a report.
H2
 What is BTL or offline marketing?
BTL (below-the-line) or offline marketing reaches specific audiences through direct, physical experiences such as
mall activations, residential society events, campus programmes, product sampling, pop-up stores, transit
branding and corporate events. Unlike mass media, BTL targets defined groups in defined places, making it ideal
for trials, launches and local demand.

 Frequently asked questions
H3
How do you measure the ROI of offline marketing?
We set a target before the activation, then track results through QR scans, unique codes, WhatsApp
opt-ins, tablet lead capture and sales data. Everyone who engages is retargeted online, so we can
measure conversions that happen days later, not just on the spot.
H3
Do you handle permissions and venue bookings?
Yes. Digi Carotene manages venue negotiations, mall and society permissions, and required local
approvals, along with fabrication, staffing and logistics, so you deal with one team.
H3
How far in advance should I plan an activation?
Ideally three to six weeks for mall, residential and campus activations, and six to ten weeks for large
events, metro branding or festival campaigns, since premium slots and dates book out early.
