H2
 The commute is the one ad slot people can't skip
Hyderabad Metro carries around 4.5 lakh passengers a day across 69 km, with Ameerpet, Miyapur, Raidurg and
HITEC City among its busiest stations. Bengaluru's Namma Metro, India's second-largest network at about 96 km
and 83 stations, averaged over 9 lakh riders a day in early 2026 and has crossed 10 lakh on peak days. That is a
captive, largely urban, working-age audience, many of them right in the IT corridors where purchasing power is
highest.
H2
 What is metro branding?
Metro branding is transit advertising on a city's metro rail network, including station branding, pillar and
concourse panels, digital screens, platform displays, inside-train panels and full or partial train wraps. It offers
high-frequency, repeated exposure to commuters along specific routes, making it effective for building recall and
targeting specific neighbourhoods and business districts.

H2
 Hyderabad Metro vs Namma Metro: which fits?
Hyderabad Metro's three corridors connect the IT hub around HITEC City and Raidurg with residential belts in
Miyapur, LB Nagar and Nagole, making it strong for tech-professional and family audiences. Namma Metro's
Purple, Green and Yellow lines link Whitefield, Electronic City, the CBD and major residential areas, and are
especially strong for reaching Bangalore's tech workforce. Many brands run both for a two-city launch.
H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How much does metro advertising cost in Hyderabad or Bangalore?
Costs depend on the format, number of stations, station traffic and campaign duration. A single-station
panel campaign costs far less than a train wrap or multi-station takeover. We share options and rates
after understanding your goals and target stations.
H3
Which metro stations are best for advertising?
For Hyderabad, high-traffic interchanges and IT corridor stations such as Ameerpet, Raidurg, HITEC City
and Miyapur deliver the most footfall. In Bangalore, Majestic and stations along the Purple Line and
Yellow Line serving Whitefield and Electronic City are popular. The best choice depends on where your
audience lives and works.
H3
How long should a metro campaign run?
Most metro campaigns run for at least one to three months to build recall through repeated exposure.
Short, high-impact bursts of two to four weeks work for launches and festive offers.
