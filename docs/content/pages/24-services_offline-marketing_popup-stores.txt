H2
 What is a pop-up store?
A pop-up store is a temporary retail space, open for a few days to a few months, that brands use to launch
products, test new markets, sell seasonal collections, create brand experiences and collect customer data.
Pop-ups can be set up in malls, high streets, cafes, co-working spaces, events or standalone structures.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How long should a pop-up store run?
Launch and buzz-focused pop-ups usually run one to three days. Market-testing and seasonal pop-ups
typically run two to eight weeks, which is long enough to collect reliable sales data and build repeat visits.
H3
How much does a pop-up store cost?
Costs depend on location rent, size, duration, fabrication and staffing. Modular builds keep costs down
and can be reused across cities. We share options at different budgets after understanding your goals.
H3
Is a pop-up worth it for a D2C brand?
Often, yes. Pop-ups let customers touch and try products, which builds trust that is hard to create online.
They also generate content, reviews and a customer list for future online marketing.
