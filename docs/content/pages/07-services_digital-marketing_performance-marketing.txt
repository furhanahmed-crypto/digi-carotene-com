H2
 What is performance marketing?
Performance marketing is paid advertising that is planned, optimised and paid for against measurable outcomes
such as leads, sales, app installs or bookings, rather than impressions. It runs on platforms like Google Ads, Meta
(Facebook and Instagram), YouTube and LinkedIn, with success measured by cost per acquisition (CPA) and
return on ad spend (ROAS).

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
What is a good ROAS for Google or Meta ads in India?
It depends on margins. A business with 60% margins can grow profitably at a 2x return on ad spend, while
a thin-margin retailer may need 5x or more. Digi Carotene calculates your break-even ROAS and target
cost per acquisition before launching, so every campaign is judged against your real economics.
H3
What is the minimum ad budget you recommend?
For local lead generation in Hyderabad or Bangalore, most businesses need enough budget to get at least
30 to 50 conversions a month so the platforms can optimise. We suggest a starting budget after the audit,
based on your industry's typical cost per lead.
H3
Google Ads or Meta Ads: which is better for my business?
Google Ads captures people already searching for your service, so it suits high-intent needs like clinics,
repairs and B2B. Meta Ads creates demand through visuals and targeting, so it suits food, fashion, beauty,
real estate and events. Most growing businesses use both, with budget shifted by results.
H3
How fast will performance marketing show results?
Leads usually start within the first week. The first two to four weeks are a learning phase where we test
audiences and creatives. Cost per lead typically stabilises and improves from the second month onwards.
