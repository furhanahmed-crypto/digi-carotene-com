H2
 When more ad spend stops working
Every scaling brand hits the same wall. Ad costs climb, conversion rates flatten, and the only lever left seems to
be "spend more". The problem is rarely the ads. It is the landing page that loses 70% of visitors, the onboarding
that confuses, the customers who buy once and disappear. Growth marketing fixes the leaks between the ad and
the repeat purchase.
H2
 What is growth marketing?
Growth marketing is a data-driven approach that improves every stage of the customer journey (acquisition,
activation, retention, referral and revenue) through rapid, measured experiments. Unlike campaign-based
marketing, it treats growth as a continuous system and prioritises the tests most likely to increase customer
lifetime value at a lower acquisition cost.

H3
What is the difference between growth marketing and performance marketing?
Performance marketing focuses on acquiring customers through paid ads at a target cost. Growth
marketing covers the entire funnel, including conversion, retention, referrals and pricing, and uses
continuous experiments to make each customer more valuable and cheaper to acquire. Digi Carotene
often runs both together.
H3
How many experiments do you run each month?
It depends on your traffic and team capacity. Most clients run four to eight meaningful experiments a
month. Low-traffic sites run fewer, larger tests so results are statistically reliable rather than random.
H3
Is growth marketing only for startups?
No. Any business with an existing flow of visitors or leads can benefit. Restaurants improving repeat
orders, clinics improving appointment show-up rates and colleges improving application completion are
all growth marketing problems.
