H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
How far in advance should we plan a corporate event?
For events of 100 to 300 guests, start six to eight weeks ahead. Large conferences, launches and award
nights need three to six months for venue booking, speakers and production. We can also execute shorter
timelines when needed.
H3
Do you handle event marketing and registrations?
Yes. We manage event websites, registration pages, email and WhatsApp invites, paid promotion,
attendee communication and post-event follow-ups, along with on-ground production.
H3
Can you cover the event with photos and videos?
Yes. Our in-house production team covers events with photography, video, live reels and highlight films,
so you have content for social media, PR and internal communications.
