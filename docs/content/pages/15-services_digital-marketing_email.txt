 What is email and WhatsApp marketing?
Email and WhatsApp marketing is the use of permission-based messages, often automated, to nurture leads,
onboard new customers, recover abandoned carts, remind people to rebook and announce offers. In India,
WhatsApp typically delivers much higher open and response rates than email, while email remains strong for
B2B, newsletters and detailed content.

H3
Is WhatsApp marketing allowed in India?
Yes, when done through the official WhatsApp Business Platform with customer opt-in and approved
message templates. Bulk messaging from personal or unofficial tools violates WhatsApp's policies and can
get numbers banned. Digi Carotene sets up compliant WhatsApp Business API accounts.

H3
Is email marketing still effective in India?
Yes, especially for B2B, education, e-commerce and content-led brands. Email is ideal for detailed
information, newsletters and long nurture sequences, while WhatsApp suits quick, transactional and
time-sensitive messages. Most brands get the best results using both.
H3
How much does WhatsApp Business API cost?
WhatsApp charges per message based on the message category (marketing, utility or authentication) and
country, in addition to any platform provider fees. We help you estimate costs based on your volumes
before setup.
