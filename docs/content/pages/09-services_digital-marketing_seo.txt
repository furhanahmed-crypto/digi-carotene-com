H2
 What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) improves your rankings in Google's regular results and Maps. AEO (Answer
Engine Optimization) structures your content so AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants can quote
it directly. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) increases how often AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and
Perplexity mention and recommend your brand. You need all three in 2026.

 Frequently asked questions
H3
How long does SEO take to work?
Technical fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can show results in 4 to 8 weeks. Ranking for
competitive keywords and seeing steady organic leads usually takes 3 to 6 months, depending on your
website's age, authority and competition in Hyderabad or Bangalore.
H3
How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?
AI tools recommend brands they see described consistently across trusted sources. Make sure your
website clearly states who you are, what you do and where; keep your details identical on Google
Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories; earn reviews and press mentions; and publish answer-first
content to the questions customers ask.
H3
Do I still need SEO if people use AI search?
Yes. AI answer engines mostly draw on pages that already rank well and are trusted. Strong SEO is the
foundation; AEO and GEO build on it so you appear both in the regular results and in the AI-generated
answer.
H3
How much do SEO services cost in Hyderabad?
SEO pricing depends on the number of locations, pages, competition and whether content and
development are included. Digi Carotene offers monthly retainers with a fixed scope agreed after a free
audit, with clear deliverables and the metrics we will report against.
H3
What is local SEO?
Local SEO helps your business appear in Google Maps and "near me" results in a specific area. It includes
optimising your Google Business Profile, building reviews, keeping your name, address and phone
consistent across listings, and creating location-specific pages.
