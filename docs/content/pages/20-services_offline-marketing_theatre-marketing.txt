H2
 What is theatre marketing?
Theatre marketing (cinema advertising) is the use of movie theatres to reach audiences through on-screen ads
played before and during films, lobby and foyer branding, digital screens, ticket and F&B branding, and
experiential activations. Brands can target specific screens, localities, languages and movies, making cinema
effective for both mass and hyper-local campaigns.

H2
 Frequently asked questions
H3
Is cinema advertising effective for local businesses?
Yes. Because you can choose specific theatres and screens, a restaurant, clinic, showroom or real estate
project can target audiences within a few kilometres of its location. Adding a QR code or local offer makes
the response measurable.
H3
Can I choose which movies my ad plays with?
In many cases, yes. You can target by language and often by specific films or genres, which is useful for
aligning with big releases or audiences such as families, youth or premium viewers.
H3
How long should a cinema ad be?
Most cinema ads run 10, 20 or 30 seconds. Shorter spots suit reminder and offer messages, while 30 to 60
seconds suits launches and storytelling. We recommend length based on your goal and budget.
