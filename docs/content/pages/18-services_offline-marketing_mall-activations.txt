H2
 What is a mall activation?
A mall activation is a short-term branded experience set up inside a shopping mall, such as a kiosk, sampling
counter, interactive booth or atrium event, designed to engage shoppers directly, drive product trials, capture
leads and create social buzz. Mall activations work best for consumer, retail, food, beauty, real estate,
automotive and fintech brands.

 Mall activations in Hyderabad and Bangalore
Hyderabad's malls draw very different crowds, from IT professionals around Hitech City and Gachibowli to

 Frequently asked questions
H3
How do you track the ROI of a mall activation?
Every engagement is captured through QR scans, WhatsApp opt-ins, unique offer codes or tablet sign-ups,
each tagged by mall and day. We report leads, cost per lead and redemptions, then retarget all engaged
shoppers online to capture conversions that happen later.

H3
Do you handle mall permissions?
Yes. We manage slot bookings, mall approvals, insurance and fabrication guidelines, and coordinate
directly with mall marketing teams so you do not have to.
H3
How long does a mall activation usually run?
Most mall activations run over a weekend (two to three days) or across consecutive weekends. Lead
generation booths for real estate or education often run two to four weeks for consistent volume.
H3
How much does a mall activation cost?
Costs depend on the mall, location within the mall, duration, booth size, fabrication and staffing. After
understanding your goals, we share options at different budgets with expected engagement and lead
estimates.
